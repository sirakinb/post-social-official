# Documentation plan

Status: draft for Aki, 2026-10-22. Written after reading the docs pages and the code they describe. Nothing here is shipped except the quickstart (item 1).

## Where docs stand today

- `/docs` is one developer-oriented page: Connect an AI, Command line, REST API, Tools (generated from `backend/lib/api/operations.ts`), Good to know.
- `/docs/billing` covers accounts, X posts and invoices. Its numbers come from `backend/lib/pricing.ts`, so they stay in step with the bill.
- `/llms.txt` is generated by `src/lib/api-docs.ts` from the same operations list.
- No sidebar, no search, no per-platform pages, no concepts page, no troubleshooting page, no recipes.
- The tools list and `/llms.txt` can't drift from the server. Hand-written pages can. The plan below leans on generating facts from code wherever possible.

## Proposed structure, ranked

Value = how much it helps a new creator get to a first working post, or cuts support questions. Effort = writing plus verification.

| Rank | Section | Value | Effort | Why this order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Quickstart for creators (a) | Highest | Low | Every new user needs it. Shipped as `/docs/quickstart`, with screenshot placeholders. |
| 2 | Troubleshooting and error reference (e) | High | Medium | Failures are where users get stuck. Most reasons already exist as plain sentences in code, so this is mostly collecting and grouping them. |
| 3 | Platform guides (c), 8 pages | High | Medium-high | Limits and quirks differ per platform. Much of it can be generated from `CAPABILITIES` and `validate.ts`; quirks need hand-writing. |
| 4 | Setup guides per AI app (g) | High | Low-medium | Claude and ChatGPT steps exist on `/docs`. Needs screenshots, and per-app checks that each flow still works. |
| 5 | Concepts (b) | Medium-high | Low | Short glossary-style page that the other pages link to. |
| 6 | Permissions and safety (f) | Medium-high | Medium | Important for trust, but blocked on the approval questions below. |
| 7 | Recipes and prompts (d) | Medium | Medium | Needs each recipe tried against the real tools so the prompts work. |
| 8 | Navigation: sidebar, search, next steps (i) | Medium | Medium-high | Gets more valuable as page count grows. Do the sidebar once there are about 6 pages. Search can wait. |
| 9 | Rate limits, status, changelog links (h) | Low-medium | Low | Changelog exists at `/changelog`. A status page link needs a status page to exist. |

Suggested order of work: 1, then 5 and 2 together, then 3, then 4, then 6 and 7, then 8 once the page count justifies it. Do the minimum navigation now (a link from `/docs` and the docs header, done) and the full sidebar later.

## Facts to verify before writing each section

What I confirmed in code while preparing the quickstart is marked "seen". Everything else must be re-checked at writing time.

### Concepts (b)
- Workspace, profile, account: a workspace holds profiles (a business, organisation or client, up to a limit set in `profiles.ts`) and connected accounts. Confirm the profile limit before stating a number. Seen: profiles are optional groupings; deleting one leaves its accounts connected.
- Post states (seen in `operations.ts`): draft, awaiting_approval, approved, scheduled, processing, published, partially_published, failed, cancelled. A post made without a time is only a draft (seen).
- Test and live keys (seen): test keys can do everything except publish or schedule.
- Approvals: see the open questions.

### Platform guides (c)
Generate limits from `CAPABILITIES` in `backend/lib/connections/platforms.ts` and the checks in `backend/lib/publishing/validate.ts` where possible. Seen so far:
- Instagram: image, reel, carousel (2 to 10 files); caption up to 2,200; Reels 3 s to 15 min; business or creator account needed.
- Facebook Pages: text, link, image, reel (3 to 90 s, 9:16), video; connects Pages, not profiles.
- Threads: text, image, video, carousel (2 to 20); 500 characters; JPEG or PNG images.
- YouTube: Shorts only, up to 3 minutes, vertical or square; title required, up to 100 characters.
- TikTok: video or photo (1 to 35 images); direct or inbox delivery; direct posts need an audience choice.
- LinkedIn: member's own profile only; 3,000 characters; up to 20 images; MP4 up to 30 minutes and 500 MB.
- Bluesky: 300 characters; up to 4 images; MP4 up to 3 minutes and 100 MB.
- X: 280 characters (links count as 23, emoji as 2); up to 4 images; video up to 2 min 20 s and 512 MB.
Still to verify: current platform review status (some platforms limit unreviewed apps, for example TikTok's private-only rule before audit), token lifetimes and reconnect cadence (for example LinkedIn tokens last about 60 days when no refresh token is returned), and which platforms offer stats.

### Troubleshooting (e)
- Collect plain-language reasons from `validate.ts`, `publishing/service.ts`, `worker/src/publish/*.ts` and `worker/src/publish/support.ts` (which marks the ones a person can fix).
- Error codes: REST errors are `{"error":{"code","message"}}`. List the real codes from `backend/lib/api/rest.ts` rather than guessing; HTTP statuses seen in code include 400, 402 (plan limit), 403, 404, 409, 413, 422, 429, 502, 503.
- Failure codes in `support.ts` (for example `x_cap_reached`, `account_disconnected`, `youtube_quota`) need a message and a fix each, taken from the adapters.

### Platform-specific quirks to confirm with a real test
- TikTok direct versus inbox: how the account setting interacts with a per-post `delivery_mode` (seen: an account set to inbox refuses direct posts).
- X: the cap, the free first dollar, and what the result says when the cap is reached. Use `/docs/billing` as the source and link to it. Don't restate numbers on the platform page.

### Permissions and safety (f)
- Approvals: see the open questions. Do not publish this page until they are answered.
- Disconnecting: accounts are disconnected on the Accounts page (also cancels scheduled posts for that account, seen); AI apps and keys are managed on the API/MCP page (seen). Confirm exactly what revoking does on each platform.
- "Never signs in for you" (seen): accounts are only connected by a person.

### Setup guides (g)
- Re-run each app's flow before writing: Claude custom connector, ChatGPT developer mode, `claude mcp add`, Cursor config. App menus change often, so date each guide.

### Limits and rate limits (h)
- Plan limits live in the `plans` table and rate limits in `backend/lib/rate-limit.ts`. Seen: per-minute request caps per key and per connection, a daily API call cap per plan, a monthly post cap per plan, and a manual stats refresh cap. Do not publish any number until Aki confirms they are meant to be public and match production, because the plan table in migrations may differ from the live database.
- Status page: none exists. Needs a decision before any link is written.

## Inconsistencies found in the code and existing docs

These are not fixed here. Each needs a decision from Aki.

1. X post price. `backend/lib/pricing.ts` charges customers 2¢ per post and 25¢ with a link, and the billing page says so. But the X notes in `CAPABILITIES` and the `create_post` options text (read by AIs through `list_social_accounts` and the tools list) say "about 1.5 cents; 20 cents with a link", which is Post Social's own cost. An AI can therefore quote the wrong price to a user.
2. MCP server instructions (`backend/lib/api/mcp.ts`) list only five platforms (Instagram, Facebook Pages, Threads, YouTube Shorts, TikTok) and say TikTok posts "go to the creator's TikTok inbox". The tools text and `/docs` say TikTok posts go direct by default, and eight platforms are supported.
3. The billing page describes the price as "1 to 10 at $5 minus a $10 monthly credit", while the pricing page says "3 to 10 at $5". They are equivalent for the bill, but readers may think they conflict. `/llms.txt` uses the billing wording.
4. `/docs/billing` still says "Questions or more than 2,000 accounts" with a support email. This may read like an enterprise hint. The 2,000 figure comes from the Paid plan's account cap in `20261021090000_open-signup-plans.sql`.
5. Free-plan wording: the Free plan caps connected accounts at 2 in the database, while the pricing and billing pages describe adding a 3rd account with a card. Confirm the plan switch happens when a card is added, as the migration comment says.

## Open questions for Aki

1. Approvals. In code the default is that AI posts go out as directed (`autonomous`), and a post waits for approval only if an account is set to ask first. I found backend support for approval requests and an error message that points people to "Approvals" in the app, but I did not find a page or setting in the web app for it. Is ask-first available to users today? If not, the safety guide should describe disconnecting and key controls only.
2. Should plan and rate-limit numbers be public? See section (h).
3. Is there a status page, or should we add one before linking it?
4. Screenshots: who captures them, and in which theme? The quickstart has marked placeholders.
5. Do you want the existing `/docs` content split into a developer section now, or after the new pages exist?
