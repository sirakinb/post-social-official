Aki, 2026-10-20 (landing page review): "you should be using your agent or AI agent, not just AI alone."

**Why:** the positioning is "AI-native creators and operators + their agents" ([[marketing-positioning]]); bare "AI" reads vague.
**How to apply:** in landing, email and marketing copy write "AI agent", "your agent", or name the app (Claude, ChatGPT). "AI-native" in the tagline stays. Screenshots on the landing carry a small source badge (Claude / Post Social) above the frame. See [[landing-page-direction]].
