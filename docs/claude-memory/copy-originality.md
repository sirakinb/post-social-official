Aki, 2026-10-20 (Billing page): after a Zernio-style rebuild he said "WE LITERALLY COPIED ZERNIO EXACTLY, can we have some sort of originality". Earlier the same day: Zernio is "subtle", shows billing info + invoices, and never says things like "X charges us"; "pay by the day" is bad language — say "your first two accounts are free, after that it's usage based, you pay per account".

**Why:** Post Social has to feel like its own product, not a clone.
**How to apply:** take the *function* from a competitor screenshot (what info is shown), then design our own layout and our own words. Billing now uses "Your month" (account dots), "Your rate" (price ladder), "X spending", "Card and receipts", "Cancel paid plan" (PR 68). Avoid Zernio terms: "Upcoming invoice", "Cost controls", "Payment method", "Billing details", "Switch to Free". Never mention proration or what X charges us.
