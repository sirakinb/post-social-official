Aki's design taste (stated 2026-10-10, while reviewing the Logs page): "organized, structured, militant, simple, minimalistic kind of vibes." He called a cluttered filter/selector area "ugly and all over the place." Apply this to any Post Social UI work.

Product rule, same date: any user-facing error a customer can't solve on their own (e.g. platform credits running out) must not surface the raw internal reason; it should tell the user to reach out to support. This applies to all errors, not just the X credits one.
